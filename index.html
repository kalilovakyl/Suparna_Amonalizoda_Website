About LINGUAÉ

A little more room
to find your voice.

At LINGUAÉ, you have time to practise, ask questions, and make mistakes without feeling embarrassed. We use conversations, short activities, and topics from everyday life to help you get comfortable speaking English.

Our lessons are open to teenagers, university students, and adults. Whether you’re working toward better grades, preparing for work, or getting ready to travel, you can start at your current level and build from there.

How we teach

What a lesson looks like.

We use examples from real situations so you can take what you learn into your next conversation. You’ll get feedback and time to practise, so you can see your progress step by step.

  1. Start with a short warm-up conversation
  2. Explore a grammar or vocabulary topic
  3. Try a few exercises to check understanding
  4. Put the lesson into practice by speaking
  5. Wrap up with feedback and a little homework
